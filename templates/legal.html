Privacy Notice

How {{ settings.site_name }} handles your information. Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

Your name, email, phone number and country when you open an account; identity documents when you complete verification; and a record of your deposits, withdrawals, investments and trades so we can run your account.

How we use it

To operate your account, confirm your identity, process payments, send security codes and account notices, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Email and security codes

We email one-time codes to confirm your address, finish sign-in when you turn on sign-in codes, and reset a forgotten password. Codes expire after 10 minutes. You can switch notification emails off in Settings.

Keeping it safe

Passwords are stored hashed, sessions are encrypted in transit, and staff access is logged. Please keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.

Your choices

You can update your details in Settings or ask us to correct or delete your information by writing to {{ settings.support_email }}.
