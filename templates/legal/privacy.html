Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 2026 • Elvarion Holdings Ltd.

1. Who We Are

Elvarion Holdings Ltd ("Elvarion," "we," "our," or "us") is a privately held multi-asset fiduciary investment management firm. We operate under institutional private wealth management charters and are committed to protecting the personal information entrusted to us by our clients.

Registered Office: Tower 49, 12 East 49th Street, New York, NY 10017.

2. Information We Collect

  • Identity data — full name, date of birth, government-issued identification numbers.
  • Contact data — email address, telephone number, residential and corporate address.
  • Financial data — source of wealth declarations, estimated net worth, transaction history, deposit and withdrawal records.
  • KYC/AML documentation — identification document scans, proof-of-address documents, selfie verification images.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, session identifiers, and access timestamps.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data solely for the following purposes:

  • To establish, maintain, and administer your investor account and custody relationship.
  • To fulfill AML/KYC regulatory obligations and accredited investor verification requirements.
  • To process investment allocations, loan applications, deposits, and withdrawals.
  • To communicate material account notices, regulatory disclosures, and portfolio updates.
  • To protect against fraud, unauthorised access, and financial crime.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with:

  • Our custodial banking partner (JPMorgan Chase & Co.) for account segregation and wire processing.
  • Regulatory and law enforcement authorities where legally required.
  • Lloyds of London underwriters for insurance and custodial compliance verification.

5. Data Security

All client data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised Elvarion compliance personnel only. We conduct periodic security audits against SOC 2 Type II standards.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact our Compliance Desk at concierge@elvarionholdings.com.
