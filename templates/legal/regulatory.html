Important Regulatory Notice

Elvarion Holdings Ltd operates as a private fiduciary investment management firm. Our services are made available solely to accredited investors, qualified institutional buyers, and eligible professional clients as defined under applicable securities regulations. Investment in our products involves risk, including the possible loss of principal.

Institutional Charter & Registration

Elvarion Holdings Ltd is a privately incorporated fiduciary wealth management and private credit firm registered under applicable private placement and institutional investment exemptions. Our operations are structured to comply with relevant AML/KYC regulations in each jurisdiction in which we accept client investments.

AML / KYC Policy

We maintain a comprehensive Anti-Money Laundering and Know-Your-Customer compliance program. All clients are required to undergo identity verification before accessing investment services or private credit facilities. We screen all clients against OFAC, UN, and relevant sanctions lists. Suspicious activity is reported to appropriate regulatory authorities as required by law.

OFAC Screened UN Sanctions Compliant PEP Screening Active SOC 2 Type II Audited

Custodial & Insurance Arrangements

Client fiat currency assets are custodied in segregated, ring-fenced accounts at JPMorgan Chase Private Bank, completely isolated from Elvarion's operational capital. Physical gold bullion holdings are specifically allocated with serial numbers in bonded Swiss vaults (Zurich and Geneva) and are comprehensively insured under Lloyds of London underwriting policies.

Risk Disclosures

  • All investment products carry inherent risk. Past performance does not guarantee future results.
  • ROI projections are indicative and based on institutional benchmark models; actual returns may differ.
  • Real estate, gold, trust fund, and money market investments are subject to market volatility, liquidity risk, and macroeconomic factors.
  • Private credit facilities are underwritten against verified deposit collateral; default on repayment may result in facility termination.
  • Cryptocurrency deposit channels carry blockchain network risks including confirmation delays and transaction finality timelines.
