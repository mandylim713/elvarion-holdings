{% include 'partials/legal/_section.html' with heading='3. How We Use Your Information' %}

We process your personal data solely for the following purposes:

  • To establish, maintain, and administer your investor account and custody relationship.
  • To fulfill AML/KYC regulatory obligations and accredited investor verification requirements.
  • To process investment allocations, loan applications, deposits, and withdrawals.
  • To communicate material account notices, regulatory disclosures, and portfolio updates.
  • To protect against fraud, unauthorised access, and financial crime.
{% include 'partials/legal/_section.html' with heading='4. Data Sharing & Third Parties' %}

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data is shared only with:

  • Our custodial banking partner (JPMorgan Chase & Co.) for account segregation and wire processing.
  • Regulatory and law enforcement authorities where legally required.
  • Lloyds of London underwriters for insurance and custodial compliance verification.
{% include 'partials/legal/_section.html' with heading='5. Data Security' %}

All client data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256. Access to personal data is restricted to authorised Elvarion compliance personnel only. We conduct periodic security audits against SOC 2 Type II standards.
